## Before you start

Sign in as an administrator for the affected organization and open **Help**. Choose documentation for guidance, an in-app request for a conversation in the workspace, or email support for an email-based request.

Gather the feature name, time of the problem, the task you attempted, and any relevant record, order, invoice, or message reference. Before retrying a payment, refund, or send, inspect its current result so your report does not create duplicate activity.

## Steps

### Send an in-app request

1. Open **Help → Notify platform owner**. This contacts the team responsible for the service; replies appear in the app.
2. Enter a specific subject, such as “Scheduled welcome email is still pending.” In the message, include:
   1. The feature and activity or record reference.
   2. The time and time zone, for example `2026-10-08 09:00 UTC`.
   3. The steps needed to reproduce the problem.
   4. Expected and actual results, the exact error, and what you already tried.
3. Attach screenshots when helpful. Use JPEG, PNG, GIF, or WebP, up to five files of 5 MB each. Show the relevant controls and error; crop unrelated information and remove passwords, verification/recovery codes, credentials, and payment details.
4. Select **Send** and check the success message. The app opens your request's conversation. Keep that page available for follow-up.
5. If submission fails, keep your description, read the error, and check whether a conversation was created before trying again.

### Follow up in the same ticket

1. Check the notification bell for ticket updates and open your request with the same administrator account.
2. Read the reply and add the requested detail or a new reproducible result. For example, report whether the same activity is now processing rather than creating another send.
3. Select **Send reply** and confirm your message appears in the conversation. Keep follow-up for the same issue in this ticket.

### Email support

1. Open **Help → Email support**.
2. Enter the subject and description. Attach images or PDF files if needed, up to five files of 5 MB each.
3. Select **Send email** and check the result notice. The request is sent to `help@anysys.com`; it does not open an in-app ticket conversation. Continue email follow-up in the email thread.
4. If you cannot sign in, email `help@anysys.com` directly from your account's email address and identify your organization. Do not send passwords or recovery codes.

## What to expect

An in-app request belongs to the administrator who sent it. Other administrators in the same organization do not automatically gain access to that conversation. Notification entries help you return to your own ticket and distinguish support updates from announcements.

Support can check missing modules, managed sending limits, supplied shared forms and record definitions, unavailable service setup, and issues you cannot resolve through customer settings. State the desired customer outcome rather than requesting access to service administration tools. A successful submission confirms receipt of the request, not that the reported problem is already fixed.

## Troubleshooting

- **An attachment is rejected:** check type, size, and count; reduce or replace it.
- **Sending fails:** read the error and confirm whether a request was created before resending. Use direct email if the in-app channel remains unavailable.
- **A ticket link cannot be opened:** use the same administrator account and organization that created it.
- **The issue changes:** reply to the existing conversation with the new result, rather than starting another report for the same problem.
