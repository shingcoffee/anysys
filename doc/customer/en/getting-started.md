## Before you start

Use the email address associated with your administrator account. If you are joining an existing organization, ask its administrator to arrange your access. Creating a new customer account and registering as a member of an existing organization are different actions.

You need access to your email inbox for email sign-in or recovery. If two-step verification is required for your account, have your authenticator available too.

## Steps

### Sign in and check your organization

1. Open the sign-in page for your organization. Use the available password or email-link option with your administrator email.
2. Complete any email confirmation or two-step verification requested on screen.
3. Check the organization shown in the workspace before opening records or changing settings. A member account opens the member experience instead.

**Check:** you have reached the intended organization's admin workspace. If the organization or access is wrong, ask its administrator to check your account.

### Find tools and return to Help

1. Open **Home** for an overview. **AI Assistant** is also an admin landing surface; use other menus if it is unavailable.
2. Expand **Modules** to see enabled features. Open a module's **How to use** introduction for links to its records or settings.
3. Open **Settings** for preferences and service billing.
4. Open **Help → Documentation** to return to these guides. Use the language control to choose English or Simplified Chinese.

![Workspace Help menu with the Documentation entry.](/help/docs/screenshots/en/workspace-help.png "Open Help, then Documentation, to return to the customer guides.")

### Complete a first task

1. Choose one small task: open **Users** to check a colleague, **Domains** to review records, or **Content → Forms** to find a supplied form. **Content → Website Pages** appears when Website is enabled.
2. Follow the corresponding guide. For example, search Users for a known colleague's email and open the matching account.
3. Confirm the name and role on the details page. Simply opening a list or details page does not save a change.

### Review your own account security

1. Open the account menu and select **Profile**, **Change password**, or **MFA setup** for the task you need.
2. Complete the screen's save or verification action and check its result. Keep any MFA recovery codes in a private place.
3. Sign out when you finish on a shared device.

## What to expect

Menus and record types vary with your organization's setup. Empty lists can be normal before your team creates records or sends messages. A missing module entry does not mean your data has been deleted.

Members use their own profile, security settings, and enabled account services. They do not receive access to these admin guides or to other members' records.

## Troubleshooting

- **An email link does not work:** request a fresh link through the appropriate sign-in or recovery screen. Check spam and use the most recent message.
- **You cannot open an admin page:** confirm that your account has administrator access in this organization. Ask an existing administrator or support to check it.
- **A feature is missing:** review **Modules**, then contact support with the feature name. Availability is managed for your organization.
- **A saved bookmark fails:** reopen the destination from the current workspace menu before reporting the problem.
