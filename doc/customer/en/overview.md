## Before you start

These guides are for customer administrators. Sign in to your organization's admin workspace, then open **Help → Documentation**. Documentation is available in English and Simplified Chinese; use the language switcher to change languages.

Administrators manage their own organization's users, records, content, and settings. Members sign in to their own account to manage their profile and use the member services made available to them.

## Steps

### Choose a goal and follow its procedure

1. Choose a task row in the grouped directory above, or use **Contents**. On a narrow screen, open **Contents** first. All guide groups are already visible, in the same order as the top menu (**Home**, **Users**, **Payments**, and the rest); scroll the list and select a short task label. New to the workspace? Start with [Getting started](getting-started).
2. Read **Before you start**, then follow the numbered steps for your task. For example, prepare an email's content and audience before submitting a send activity.
3. Check the result beside the procedure. Saving, publishing, and sending are different actions; each guide explains the distinction.
4. On an article, use **Previous** / **Next** near the title or at the bottom to continue in guide order. Both lead to the same guides. **More in this section** offers other guides in the current group. Select a group in the breadcrumb to return to that part of the directory, or **All guides** to open the guide home. **Help → Documentation** always opens this page.

### Search without losing your place

1. Enter a word or phrase in the Contents search field, then select **Search** or press Enter. Search narrows the guide list only; the current article and complete home directory stay available.
2. Read **Search results** to choose a matching guide. **Reading now** above the results keeps your current group and guide visible, including when nothing matches. The home link and search controls stay above the scrolling list.
3. Select **Clear search** to restore the full list while staying on the same page and in the same language. Selecting the guide under **Reading now** also clears search without changing articles. Group links, **More in this section**, and both sets of **Previous** / **Next** remain available during search; following them opens the destination without the search filter.

## What to expect

Your menus reflect the modules enabled for your organization. **Modules** contains available features and their **How to use** guides; **Settings** contains their customer settings. Some supplied content and service limits are managed for you.

## Troubleshooting

- **A feature is missing:** confirm your organization and administrator role, then check **Modules**. Ask support to check availability if the entry is still absent.
- **You cannot find a guide:** select **Clear search**, scroll the visible guide groups, choose a task row from **All guides**, or switch languages. Use [Support](support) if a guide or destination remains unavailable.
